## 1. Decision context

M4.1 Revision 4 is accepted at the design-contract level. Its C-01/C-02/P2
closure is not operational certification and does not prove compatibility with
the live MySQL/Alembic, M2.5, or Qdrant interfaces.

M4.2 is the future **forward-only metadata/memory schema and scoped repository
layer**. It may proceed only after every governance decision and compatibility
prerequisite in this document has independent, current, reviewable evidence.
The six M3.2.1 live acceptance gates remain separate and pending.

## 6. Entry decision and release criteria

**Decision: BLOCKED.** M4.2 may not begin schema planning, schema changes,
repository implementation, migration design execution, or disposable testing
until all conditions below are independently verified:

- M4-GOV-01 through M4-GOV-07 are each current `APPROVED` records with
  `readiness: VERIFIED` and evidence references;
- MySQL/Alembic compatibility is reviewed against the actual target schema;
- M2.5 interface compatibility confirms Revision 4 ownership/recovery/egress
  semantics without creating safety bypasses;
- Qdrant compatibility confirms the planned tenant filtering and future
  physical-readiness semantics for the identified target; and
- an independent reviewer records an explicit M4.2 planning-entry decision.

Even after M4.2 planning entry is accepted, a separate approval is required
before any migration, disposable test environment, Qdrant deployment,
ingestion, worker activation, or external provider egress.
