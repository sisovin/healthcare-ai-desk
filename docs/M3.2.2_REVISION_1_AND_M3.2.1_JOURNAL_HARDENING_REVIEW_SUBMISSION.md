## Journal-hardening contract submitted for review

- A `JournalFileLock` covers journal read/verify, mutation, atomic replacement, and reload/verify; stale/ambiguous lock ownership fails closed without automatic breaking.
- `PREPARED` records are persisted and reload-verified before a future authorized runner could execute a statement.
- Outcomes are append-only and must match the sole unresolved `PREPARED` record.
- `PASSED` requires every observed precondition/postcondition to be true and valid snapshot/fingerprint evidence.
- `STOP_NO_RETRY` is permanent, safe-reason-coded terminal evidence; unresolved `PREPARED` after restart requires independent reconciliation rather than automatic retry.
- The module has no SQL, Alembic, Docker, MySQL, Redis, provider, or tool invocation capability.
