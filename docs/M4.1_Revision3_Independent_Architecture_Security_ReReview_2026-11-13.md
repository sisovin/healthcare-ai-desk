## Review clarification C-01 — crash after consumption (recommended P1 before executable integration)
The contract says `CONSUMED→UNCERTAIN` on timeout/crash **when observed**, while also calling `CONSUMED` terminal. In particular, a sender may crash after durable `CONSUMED` and before the remote call or after transmission but before acknowledgement; upon restart the distinction is unknowable without provider evidence. Require an M2.5-owned reconciliation/recovery rule that conservatively treats a recovered non-final `CONSUMED` attempt as uncertain (or an equivalently blocked state), never resends it using the same decision, and requires fresh policy/operational authorization for any retry. Define whether `CONSUMED` means dispatch-attempt recorded, not provider success, and define who may append provider outcome evidence. A transition to UNCERTAIN is permitted, so avoid calling CONSUMED absolutely terminal in the sense of immutable state. Add crash-at-every-boundary tests: NOT_SENT, RESERVED, after CONSUMED-before-send, during send, after send-before-ack.

## Review clarification C-02 — staged receipts vs physical index readiness (implementation gate)
MySQL per-chunk receipts and manifest sufficiency are specified. At M4.3 implementation, specify when a point receipt is considered committed/verified against Qdrant's chosen write consistency, and how the system handles a physical point missing after a successful receipt or during snapshot/rebuild.
